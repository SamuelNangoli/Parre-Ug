# 🎟️ Parre.ug

### WE WANNA PARRE!

**Book Now. Pay Gradually.**

Parre.ug is an event ticketing platform designed to make concerts and entertainment events more accessible through flexible instalment payments. Users can discover events, secure tickets and spread payments over time instead of paying the full ticket price upfront.

---

## 🚀 About the Project

Attending concerts and entertainment events should not be limited by the ability to pay for a ticket all at once.

Parre.ug aims to bridge this gap by allowing users to book tickets and pay in manageable instalments. Once the full payment is completed, users receive a digital ticket with a QR code for event entry.

The platform also provides event organisers with tools to manage events, monitor ticket sales and track payments.

## ⚠️ The Problem

Many potential concert and event attendees cannot afford the full ticket price upfront. This limits access to entertainment and can result in lost ticket sales for event organisers.

## 💡 Our Solution

Parre.ug introduces flexible ticket payments, allowing customers to reserve their tickets and complete payments gradually before receiving their digital tickets.

## ⭐ Key Features

### For Event Goers

- **Event Discovery:** Explore upcoming concerts and entertainment events.
- **Flexible Payments:** Choose between full payment and instalment payments.
- **Booking Management:** View bookings and monitor payment progress.
- **Payment Reminders:** Receive reminders for upcoming instalments.
- **Digital Tickets:** Receive QR-code tickets after completing payment.
- **Easy Access:** Present digital tickets for scanning and verification at event entrances.

### For Event Organisers

- **Event Management:** Create, publish and manage events.
- **Ticket Management:** Configure ticket categories and pricing.
- **Sales Tracking:** Monitor ticket sales and booking activity.
- **Payment Monitoring:** Track customer instalment payments.
- **Analytics:** View event performance and ticket sales information.
- **Customer Management:** Access booking and attendee information.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Discover** – Browse available events on Parre.ug.
2. **Select** – Choose an event and preferred ticket category.
3. **Book** – Select the number of tickets and payment option.
4. **Pay** – Make an initial payment and complete the remaining instalments.
5. **Receive** – Get your digital QR ticket after full payment.
6. **Attend** – Present your ticket for scanning and verification at the event.

## 🏗️ Platform Architecture

Parre.ug is being developed around three main components:

- **Mobile Application:** The customer-facing platform for discovering events, booking tickets and managing instalment payments.
- **Backend API:** Handles authentication, event information, bookings, payment records and ticket generation.
- **Web Dashboard:** Provides event organisers with tools to manage events, monitor ticket sales and view performance.

## 🎯 Target Customers

University students, young professionals, entertainment enthusiasts, concert promoters and event organisers.

## 💰 Revenue Model

Parre.ug plans to generate revenue through:

- **Ticketing Commission:** A percentage of completed ticket sales.
- **Convenience Fees:** Small fees charged on eligible transactions.
- **Organiser Packages:** Paid packages for event organisers.
- **Promotional Services:** Paid event promotion and visibility opportunities.
